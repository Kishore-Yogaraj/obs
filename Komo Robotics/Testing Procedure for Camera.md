### USB Connectivity
Need to buy a cable because buyer doesn't have one

**Connecter**
- UBS-C to USB-C, 10Gbps, 3 meter
- USB 3.2 Gen 2 (10 Gbps)
	- USB 3.1 Gen 2 also works
- 3A / 60W power rating or higher
	- At least 5V/3A
- Supports data transfer

**USB Connection Test**
- Connect the OAK-D Pro to laptop
- Open OAK Viwer
- Check that the camera is detected and appears in the device selection interface
- Connect to it and confirm it can start a video stream
