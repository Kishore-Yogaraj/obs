This is a **Hybrid Hyper-Carry / Card-Spam Wraith** build. The goal is to scale your fire rate, on-hit spirit procs, and bounce damage so that every magazine generates a flood of **Card Tricks**, turning you into both a massive single-target assassin and a multi-target teamfight shredder.

### 1. How the Build Works (Core Mechanics & Combos)

- **Ability Order (from top-right chart):**
    
    1. **Unlock order:** Card Trick (`1`) $\rightarrow$ Project Mind (`2`) $\rightarrow$ Full Auto (`3`) $\rightarrow$ Telekinesis (`4`).
        
    2. **First Rush — Max Full Auto (`3`):** You put 1 early point in Card Trick (`1`) and Project Mind (`2`), then rush **Full Auto (`3`)** all the way to Tier 3 (5 AP). Maxed Full Auto grants Spirit Lifesteal and massive fire rate, which sustains you in duels and generates cards faster.
        
    3. **Second Rush — Max Telekinesis (`4`):** After Full Auto is maxed, dump points into **Telekinesis (`4`)** to max it out for lower cooldown and stronger lockdown, then finish **Card Trick (`1`)** and **Project Mind (`2`)**.
        
- **Key Item Synergies:**
    
    - **Surge of Power (imbue on Card Trick or Full Auto) + Quicksilver Reload (imbue on Card Trick):** Imbuing Card Trick with Quicksilver Reload means throwing a card instantly reloads your gun and grants bonus fire rate. Putting Surge of Power on Card Trick (or Full Auto) gives huge bursts of Spirit Power and move speed every time you cast.
        
    - **Ricochet + Tesla Bullets + Toxic Bullets + Capacitor:** Wraith generates Card Trick charges by dealing bullet damage. Ricochet bounces your shots to nearby enemies, proccing Tesla chain lightning and Toxic Bullets on multiple targets while refilling your cards almost instantly during fights.
        
    - **Mercurial Magnum + Boundless Spirit:** Mercurial Magnum adds flat Spirit damage to your bullets scaling off your Spirit Power. Boundless Spirit supercharges your cards, Full Auto, and Mercurial Magnum procs all at once.
        
- **Standard Execution Combo:**
    
    1. Pop **Fleetfoot** (and **Full Auto** if engaging aggressively) to close distance or flank from an off-angle.
        
    2. Lock down a priority target with **Telekinesis**.
        
    3. Unload your magazine into their head while suspended, weaving **Card Tricks** between bursts to trigger **Quicksilver Reload** so you never have to sit through a manual reload animation.
        
    4. Hold **Project Mind (`2`)** to chase a flash/dash or teleport onto high ground to escape once enemy backup arrives.
        

### 2. Your Power Spikes & What to Do When You Hit Them

#### Spike 1: **Tesla Bullets + Quicksilver Reload** (~6–8 mins / Early-to-Mid Transition)

- **Why it's a spike:** Quicksilver Reload removes your downtime in lane trades, and Tesla Bullets drastically increases your wave-clear and jungle-clearing speed.
    
- **What to do:** **Hyper-farm.** Stop taking coin-flip fights. Shove your lane wave quickly, immediately rotate into nearby neutral camps and Sinner's Sacrifice machines, and return to catch the next wave. Only fight if an enemy overextends under your tower or your jungler ganks with CC.
    

#### Spike 2: **Maxed Full Auto (5 AP) + Ricochet + Mercurial Magnum** (~15–22 mins / Core Online)

- **Why it's a spike:** This is your biggest mid-game inflection point. Maxed Full Auto gives you sustain and fire rate, Ricochet turns every camp and wave into instant souls while multiplying your card generation, and Mercurial Magnum gives you lethal 1v1 burst.
    
- **What to do:**
    
    1. **Clean out the map:** Vacuum up Tier 2/3 jungle camps in seconds with Ricochet + Tesla.
        
    2. **Hunt isolated side-laners:** Look at the minimap for enemy carries or supports pushing a side lane alone. Flank with Fleetfoot, Telekinesis them, and melt them before their team can rotate.
        
    3. **Take Objectives:** With Full Auto + Burst Fire + Mercurial Magnum, you shred Walkers and Mid-Boss faster than almost anyone in the lobby.
        

#### Spike 3: **Boundless Spirit + Toxic Bullets / Capacitor + Late-Game Tier 4s** (25+ mins / Late Game)

- **Why it's a spike:** Your cards hit like trucks, Toxic Bullets + Spirit Rend shred tanky frontliners, and Capacitor strips enemy shields/buffs while amplifying your electric damage.
    
- **What to do:** **Play disciplined teamfights—never engage first.**
    
    - Wait 2–3 seconds for your frontline to bait out enemy stuns and ultimates.
        
    - Angle in from the side, Telekinesis their backline carry or biggest teamfight threat, and delete them.
        
    - **Buy your situational defense:** If you are getting chain-stunned or focused the moment you show, pause damage items and grab **Unstoppable** (against heavy CC like Lash/Mo & Krill/Dynamo) or **Cheat Death / Ethereal Shift** from your late/situational tabs so you survive the initial burst.
