ROBOHAX Robotics Hackathon – Prize Sponsorship Opportunity

Hello Prusa Research Team,

I hope you’re doing well.

Worldwide Robotics Hub is hosting **ROBOHAX**, a robotics hackathon this fall. The event brings together people from across the robotics community, including high school and university students, graduate researchers, engineers, makers, and industry professionals.

Participants will be building robots around the **Lord of Robots Lore Core Base**, a custom ESP32-based microcontroller platform. The exact challenge and competition objectives will be revealed at the official kickoff.

We’re currently reaching out to organizations that we believe would be a strong fit for the event, and Prusa Research immediately stood out. We’ve seen Prusa’s strong commitment to education through the **Prusa Education** program and its support for students, universities, makers, and hands-on technical projects. Given the close connection between 3D printing, rapid prototyping, engineering, and robotics, we thought ROBOHAX would align especially well with the community Prusa already supports.

We are currently looking for **prize sponsors**, with a preference for in-kind sponsorship rather than cash prizes. At this stage, we are simply looking to gauge interest and see whether Prusa Research would be open to learning more about the sponsorship opportunity.

3D printing is particularly relevant to the ROBOHAX community. Robotics teams routinely rely on additive manufacturing to prototype mechanisms, sensor mounts, enclosures, structural components, and other custom hardware. Many of the students and engineers attending the event are exactly the type of builders who could continue using 3D-printing tools well beyond the hackathon.

We also noticed that Prusa has incorporated 3D printers, filament, and other products as prizes in its own educational and maker competitions. We believe that same emphasis on rewarding hands-on technical creativity makes Prusa a natural fit for ROBOHAX.

ROBOHAX provides an opportunity to connect directly with a highly engaged audience of students, engineers, robotics enthusiasts, makers, and hardware developers. Sponsorship opportunities can also include additional visibility through event booths, speaking opportunities, social media recognition, video features, and other event promotion.

If this sounds like something Prusa Research may be interested in, we would be happy to arrange a short video call and share more information about the event and the available sponsorship options.
