ROBOHAX Robotics Hackathon – Prize Sponsorship Opportunity

Hello ELEGOO Marketing Team,

I hope you’re doing well.

Worldwide Robotics Hub is hosting **ROBOHAX**, a robotics hackathon this fall. The event brings together people across the robotics community, including high school and university students, graduate researchers, engineers, and industry professionals.

Participants will be building robots around the **Lord of Robots Lore Core Base**, a custom ESP32-based microcontroller platform. The exact challenge and competition objectives will be revealed at the official kickoff.

We’re currently reaching out to organizations that we believe would be a strong fit for the event, and ELEGOO immediately stood out. We’ve seen ELEGOO’s support for schools, FIRST Robotics Competition teams, university projects, and other hands-on STEM initiatives, so we thought ROBOHAX could align well with the types of communities ELEGOO already supports.

We are currently looking for **prize sponsors**, with a preference for in-kind sponsorship rather than cash prizes. At this stage, we are simply looking to gauge interest and see whether ELEGOO would be open to learning more about the sponsorship opportunity.

ROBOHAX provides an opportunity to connect with a highly engaged audience of students, engineers, robotics enthusiasts, and builders. Sponsorship opportunities can also include additional visibility through event booths, speaking opportunities, social media recognition, video features, and other event promotion.

If this sounds like something ELEGOO may be interested in, we would be happy to arrange a short video call and share more information about the event and the available sponsorship options.
