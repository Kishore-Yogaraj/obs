ROBOHAX Robotics Hackathon – Prize Sponsorship Opportunity

Hello Pololu Team,

I hope you’re doing well.

Worldwide Robotics Hub is hosting **ROBOHAX**, a robotics hackathon this fall. The event brings together people from across the robotics community, including high school and university students, graduate researchers, engineers, makers, and industry professionals.

Participants will be building robots around the **Lord of Robots Lore Core Base**, a custom ESP32-based microcontroller platform. The exact challenge and competition objectives will be revealed at the official kickoff.

We’re currently reaching out to organizations that we believe would be a strong fit for the event, and Pololu immediately stood out. Pololu’s focus on robotics and electronics, with products ranging from motors, motor drivers, encoders, sensors, controllers, wheels, and chassis to complete robot platforms, closely aligns with the types of hardware our participants work with when building robotic systems.

We are currently looking for **prize sponsors**, with a preference for in-kind sponsorship rather than cash prizes. At this stage, we are simply looking to gauge interest and see whether Pololu would be open to learning more about the sponsorship opportunity.

We believe Pololu would be particularly relevant to the ROBOHAX community because your products are directly applicable to hands-on robotics development. Whether someone is building a mobile robot, developing a mechanism, implementing closed-loop motor control, or integrating sensors into a new system, many of the components Pololu offers can become part of their next robotics project. This makes Pololu hardware especially practical as prizes for participants who are likely to continue designing and building after the hackathon.

ROBOHAX also provides an opportunity to connect directly with a highly engaged audience of students, engineers, robotics enthusiasts, makers, and hardware developers. Sponsorship opportunities can include additional visibility through event booths, speaking opportunities, social media recognition, video features, and other event promotion.

If this sounds like something Pololu may be interested in, we would be happy to arrange a short video call and share more information about the event and the available sponsorship options.
