ROBOHAX Robotics Hackathon - Prize Sponsorship Opportunity

Hello Bambu Lab Team,

I hope you’re doing well.

Worldwide Robotics Hub is hosting **ROBOHAX**, a robotics hackathon this fall. The event brings together people from across the robotics community, including high school and university students, graduate researchers, engineers, makers, and industry professionals.

Participants will be building robots around the **Lord of Robots Lore Core Base**, a custom ESP32-based microcontroller platform. The exact challenge and competition objectives will be revealed at the official kickoff.

We’re currently reaching out to organizations that we believe would be a strong fit for the event, and Bambu Lab immediately stood out. We saw Bambu Lab’s support of the **HARD MODE MIT Hardware × AI Hackathon** earlier this year, and given the strong overlap between 3D printing, rapid prototyping, robotics, and hardware development, we thought ROBOHAX could align very well with the types of engineering and maker communities Bambu Lab supports.

We are currently looking for **prize sponsors**, with a preference for in-kind sponsorship rather than cash prizes. At this stage, we are simply looking to gauge interest and see whether Bambu Lab would be open to learning more about the sponsorship opportunity.

3D printing plays a major role in modern robotics development, particularly for quickly prototyping mechanisms, enclosures, mounts, and other custom components. Because ROBOHAX participants will be actively designing and building physical robotic systems, we believe Bambu Lab would be especially relevant to the audience attending the event.

ROBOHAX also provides an opportunity to connect directly with a highly engaged audience of students, engineers, robotics enthusiasts, makers, and hardware developers. Sponsorship opportunities can include additional visibility through event booths, speaking opportunities, social media recognition, video features, and other event promotion.

If this sounds like something Bambu Lab may be interested in, we would be happy to arrange a short video call and share more information about the event and the available sponsorship options.
