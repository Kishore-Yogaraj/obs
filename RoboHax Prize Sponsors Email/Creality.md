ROBOHAX Robotics Hackathon - Prize Sponsorship Opportunity

Hello Creality Team,

I hope you’re doing well.

Worldwide Robotics Hub is hosting **ROBOHAX**, a robotics hackathon this fall as. The event brings together people from across the robotics community, including high school and university students, graduate researchers, engineers, makers, and industry professionals.

Participants will be building robots around the **Lord of Robots Lore Core Base**, a custom ESP32-based microcontroller platform. The exact challenge and competition objectives will be revealed at the official kickoff.

We’re currently reaching out to organizations that we believe would be a strong fit for the event, and Creality immediately stood out. We’ve seen Creality’s support for STEM education and student maker communities, including its partnership with the Purdue University 3D Printing Club, where Creality sponsored printers and scanners for its student makerspace. We thought ROBOHAX could align very well with Creality’s efforts to make 3D printing more accessible to students, engineers, and makers.

We are currently looking for **prize sponsors**, with a preference for in-kind sponsorship rather than cash prizes. At this stage, we are simply looking to gauge interest and see whether Creality would be open to learning more about the sponsorship opportunity.

3D printing is especially relevant to the ROBOHAX audience because rapid prototyping is such an important part of robotics development. Participants and attendees regularly work with custom mechanisms, enclosures, sensor mounts, structural components, and other parts that benefit from additive manufacturing. We believe Creality products would therefore be particularly exciting and useful prizes for this community.

ROBOHAX also provides an opportunity to connect directly with a highly engaged audience of students, engineers, robotics enthusiasts, makers, and hardware developers. Sponsorship opportunities can include additional visibility through event booths, speaking opportunities, social media recognition, video features, and other event promotion.

If this sounds like something Creality may be interested in, we would be happy to arrange a short video call and share more information about the event and the available sponsorship options.
