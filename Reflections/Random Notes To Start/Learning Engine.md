### Below is the learning engine for conceptual understanding and memorization
Encode
- Read the slide
- Deeply understand what it's talking about with AI if needed
- Rewrite the slide in your own words and check if you forgot anything
- If you did write it in red and rewrite again
Consolidate
- Sleep
- Exercise
Storage and retrieval
- After finishing the slide show do a full recall in your notes
- If you forgot some parts after comparing your recall, do the recall again until you get it right
- Do it the next day, if you get it wrong, then do it again in the same day then do it the next day
- If you get it right then do it in 3 days. If you get it wrong, do it again immediately and do it again in 3 days
