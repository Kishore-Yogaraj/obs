### What is Neuroplasticity
The brain's ability to change and adapt.
- Your brain is not a fixed, static thing
	- We used to think that once you reach adult hood you're not able to learn or grow anymore and we are stuck with what we developed in childhood

### Why our brain isn't wired properly to learn very efficiently
#### What was the brain originally adapted to do
- Good at noticing patterns
- Good at learning things that are practical
- Good at learning things socially from other people
It's good at all of these things because the brain was developed for survival

So when you ask you brain to learn a high **volume** of information, within a short amount of **time** with a high level of **detail** when it doesn't really seem **practical** or the **patterns** are not obvious at first... You're asking your brain to do something that it **wasn't built** to do.


#### How did we figure out how to combat this?
- Rote memorization
- Repetition
- Lots of notes repetitively

We use these methods for years and they become habits of learning. We do this for years and instead of thinking about what the brain was originally adapted to do we begin thinking about the above methods when it comes to adapting our brain.

We assume that these habits is what learning is which is **not true.**

**The way everyone has been learning for years is not the way we should be learning. It's the way we have been forced to do things to survive in this system.**
