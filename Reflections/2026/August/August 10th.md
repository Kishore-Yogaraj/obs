You can control how you think and you can control what you think. Therefore, you can control who you are and what you become.

Getting passed the 3 hour mark is a battle like any other mental battle. Your brain wants you to quite. You have trained it to believe that its okay to quit after 3 hours but it isn't. Just like you trained your brain to be able to sit down and do the work, you need to be able to do the same for after 3 hours as well. It's not overloaded with information. It simply doesn't like it and it's ability to make you quit after 3 hours is clearly so powerful. Are you going to be a slave to your brain after 3 hours of studying? Are you going to give a weak brain to the 25 year old version of you? One that can't focus for more than 3 hours? Do it for him.

Forget the results and focus completely on the task at hand. Let go of success or failure. Let go of praise or blame. Let go of profit or loss. 

You are allowed to struggle and fail. It is still progress.
