**Ego is the story you tell about yourself.** It refers to a person's sense of self-esteem or self-importance. Ego is not a bad thing given that the ego is actually healthy.

A **healthy ego** fosters confidence, emotional regulation, and self respect.

An **unhealthy ego** can lead to arrogance, stubbornness or narcissism.

So why does the **grind mentality** mean your ego is out of check? Because the grind mentality puts **how you look doing the work** above **actually doing the work.**

When ego runs the show, the real goal quietly shifts. It's no longer "get this done well". It's "be seen (by yourself or others) as someone who earned it". The grind becomes point, not the output. Although enjoying the process is important, you're more worried about people seeing that you went through the process rather than actually going through the process.
